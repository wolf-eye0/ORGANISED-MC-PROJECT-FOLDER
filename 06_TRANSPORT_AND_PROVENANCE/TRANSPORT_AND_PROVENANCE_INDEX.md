# Project mC — Transport and Provenance Index

## Purpose

This directory records how files were assembled and delivered to different AI
research and synthesis systems.

It is not a technical evidence directory.

## Governing instructions

These files specify the exact task, authority order, exclusions and quality
rules used for major research, synthesis, adjudication and teacher-document
workflows.

## Input manifests

Manifests establish which physical attachments were expected. They do not prove
the technical truth of any attached document.

## Launchers and checklists

Launchers preserve the initiating prompt. Checklists preserve upload-control
steps.

## Reduced-file bundles

Gemini and Qwen bundles combine original files solely to satisfy upload-count
limits. They are transport compilations, not superior or newer project
authorities.

## Package validation

Validation records establish whether a reduced package represented its intended
source set. They are administrative evidence only.

## Deduplication policy

When an artifact's exact SHA-256 content already exists elsewhere in the
organized workspace, this stage records the existing location instead of
creating another physical copy.
