# VibeGuard Pre-Build Future Patent-Case Simulation Instructions

## Purpose

Conduct an independent pre-build hypothetical patent-case simulation for
VibeGuard alone.

This is not legal advice, a patentability opinion, claim construction,
infringement analysis, FTO clearance, filing recommendation or prediction of
patent grant.

Use fresh web research and primary patent/technical sources. Do not rely only
on the attached syntheses.

The later features are hypothetical and unbuilt. Do not assume that they work.

1. Engineering Design Review.
2. Project mC Decision Register v1.2.
3. Final VibeGuard Architecture Report.
4. Final VibeGuard Memory.
5. Final VibeGuard SOP.
6. Uncertainty and Test Register.
7. Valid VibeGuard syntheses after reliability audit.
8. Cross-concept adjudications as decision context, not votes.
9. Fresh primary-source patent and technical verification.

## Frozen semester MVP

Preserve:

- one rigidly mounted ADXL345-class accelerometer on the target machine;
- ESP32-WROOM-32 or ESP32-S3;
- local acquisition and preprocessing;
- time-domain features;
- RMS-plus-persistence minimum classifier;
- triggered FFT and band features;
- prospective Mahalanobis normal-space scoring;
- local RGB state;
- USB logging;
- isolated-motor/fan Level-1 normal-versus-induced-abnormal demonstration;
- no cloud dependency;
- no permanent second product sensor;
- no active vibration cancellation;
- no multi-sensor array;
- no RUL claim.

Treat this frozen MVP as conventional and not as the proposed invention.

Keep distinct:

- Level 1: isolated target normal versus induced abnormal.
- Level 2: target-machine attribution despite independently changing
  interference, remounting and operating-state variation.

## Hypothetical future invention space

Investigate later architectures involving materially different combinations
of:

1. independently labelled target and interferer commissioning;
2. target-conditioned and interference-conditioned fingerprints;
3. separate target-abnormality and source-ambiguity evidence;
4. explicit Unknown or abstain output;
5. candidate-buffer quarantine;
6. contamination-protected baseline admission, rollback and lockout;
7. remount detection, invalidation or bounded transfer;
8. speed/load conditioning;
9. permanent local execution on the target-mounted ESP32;
10. temporary reference instruments used only for experimental ground truth.

Do not treat FFT, Mahalanobis distance, confidence, abstention, adaptive
baseline, rollback, remount checks, edge execution or AI terminology as novel
by themselves.

Verify identifiers, priority, families, applicants, independent claims and
official status where feasible.

Distinguish prior-art relevance from FTO relevance.

Do not call FTO clear.

## Claim simulation

Draft only simulation artefacts:

- one tentative apparatus independent claim;
- one tentative method independent claim;
- dependent fallback concepts.

Then attack and narrow them.

Map every prospective claim element to a required future physical experiment.

Do not describe the claims as filing-ready.

## Strong baselines

At minimum compare future mechanisms against:

- RMS plus persistence;
- FFT/band features;
- ordinary Mahalanobis;
- static spectral subtraction;
- naive adaptive baseline;
- full recalibration after remount;
- forced binary classification without Unknown;
- each critical feature removed individually.

Cover:

- field-like or real-field validation;
- refreshed claim-focused search;

## Required final recommendation

End with exactly one:

A. Preserve and test a named prospective invention architecture.
B. Redesign the prospective mechanism before testing.
C. Complete only the semester MVP and wait for evidence.
D. Retain VibeGuard as publication/trade-secret engineering only.
E. Kill the VibeGuard patent route.

If A or B is selected, state:

- exact mechanism;
- semester choices to preserve;
- earliest decisive experiment;
- strongest baseline;
- primary kill condition;
- information impossible to know before building;
- confidentiality boundary;
- whether a separate Deep Research verification remains necessary.
