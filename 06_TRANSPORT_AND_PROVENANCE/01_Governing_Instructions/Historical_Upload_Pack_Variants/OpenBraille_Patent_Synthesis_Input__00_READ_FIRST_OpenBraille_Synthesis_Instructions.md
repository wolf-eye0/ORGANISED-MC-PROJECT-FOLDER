Treat the current OpenBraille baseline as:

- one six-dot refreshable tactile Braille cell;
- approximately 300% standard Braille scale;
- approximately 7 mm dot pitch;
- approximately 1.5 mm dot protrusion target;

classified as such.

## Evidence-review methodology

### Step 1 — Inventory verification

Before synthesis:

- list every attached file;
- classify it as authority, historical lead or independent research;
- confirm the actual number of independent reports;
- identify missing, duplicate or unreadable inputs;
- stop if a critical report is unavailable.

### Step 2 — Independent-report audit

Audit each research report separately for:

- concept fidelity;
- search breadth;
- primary-source use;
- independent-claim analysis;
- family accuracy;
- legal-status accuracy;
- jurisdiction coverage;
- citation traceability;
- hallucinated patent identifiers;
- incorrect family relationships;
- confusion between patent disclosure and claim scope;
- confusion between expired prior art and current FTO risk;
- unsupported conclusions;
- macro-scale reasoning;
- three-month feasibility reasoning.

Assign each report:

- High reliability;
- Medium reliability;
- Low reliability;
- Excluded in whole or in part.

Do not use model reputation as evidence quality.

### Step 3 — Claim-level primary verification

Independently verify the load-bearing findings.

Prefer:

- Indian Patent Office records;
- WIPO PATENTSCOPE;
- USPTO records;
- EPO Espacenet and European Patent Register;
- official Chinese, Japanese and Korean records where accessible.

Google Patents may be used for discovery and family navigation but must not
be the sole basis for a load-bearing legal-status conclusion.

For every materially important record, distinguish:

- published disclosure;
- pending application;
- granted patent;
- abandoned application;
- expired or lapsed patent;
- active claim status where verifiable;
- prior-art relevance;
- FTO relevance.

Expired or abandoned records may remain highly relevant to novelty and
inventive step even where they no longer create the same FTO concern.

If an official record cannot be accessed, mark the conclusion unresolved.

### Step 4 — Evidence conflict resolution

Resolve disagreements using:

1. verified independent claims;
2. official family and status records;
3. technical similarity to the frozen architecture;
4. corroborated non-patent prior art;
5. credible experimental evidence;
6. report reasoning quality.

Do not resolve disagreements by counting models.

A well-supported minority report may override several weak reports.

## Macro-scale and practical-scale analysis

The semester prototype is approximately 300% standard Braille scale.

For each prospective claim element classify it as:

1. Scale-independent;
2. Scale-sensitive;
3. Standard-scale validation mandatory.

Evaluate:

- whether macro-scale data meaningfully supports the element;
- what technical effects are likely to transfer;
- which effects depend on pitch, cell thickness, actuator dimensions,
  friction, force, tolerance, tactile readability or array density;
- whether the direction has useful scope when limited to macro scale;
- whether calculations, simulation, tolerance analysis, detailed drawings or
  a practical-scale prototype are needed;
- whether a standard-scale embodiment is mandatory before the direction can
  be considered technically credible.

Do not treat macro-scale operation as proof of standard-scale feasibility.

Also do not dismiss scale-independent control evidence merely because the
mechanical demonstrator is enlarged.
