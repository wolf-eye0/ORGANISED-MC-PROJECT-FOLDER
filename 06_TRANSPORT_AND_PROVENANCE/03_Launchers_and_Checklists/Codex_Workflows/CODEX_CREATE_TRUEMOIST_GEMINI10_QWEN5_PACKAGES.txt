- preserve the original source order;

- insert only a clearly marked transport header and source-boundary markers;

  `<!-- BEGIN EMBEDDED SOURCE: <filename> -->`

  `<!-- END EMBEDDED SOURCE: <filename> -->`

  markers;
- leave at least two blank lines between logical documents;
- use UTF-8 without BOM;
- do not wrap embedded documents in code fences.

- the compilation itself is not technical evidence;
- models must inventory the embedded logical documents separately.

Verify by programmatic byte/text comparison that every original source body is
embedded exactly once and unchanged.
