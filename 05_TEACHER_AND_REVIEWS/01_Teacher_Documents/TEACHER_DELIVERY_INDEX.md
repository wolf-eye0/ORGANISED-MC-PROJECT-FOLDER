- The VibeGuard roadmap becomes the controlling implementation plan only after
  the change and exact scope are approved.
- The OpenBraille dossier remains the documented fallback and retained future
  accessibility/mechatronics reference.
- Neither ordinary semester prototype is presented as patent-ready.
