# Project mC — Historical Research Index

## Purpose

This index covers independent and supporting research conducted before the
final Phase 3C concept architectures and patent-evidence packages were frozen.

## Included research levels

- Phase 1A opportunity discovery.
- Phase 1C abstract and concept formation.
- Phase 2 historical patent-landscape research.
- Phase 3A engineering-landscape research.
- Phase 3B engineering-feasibility research.

## Authority boundary

These files explain the path followed by Project mC. They do not override:

1. teacher requirements;
2. Decision Register v1.2;
3. approved final Phase 3C architectures;
4. final memories and SOPs;
5. current comparative evidence;
6. controlled patent synthesis and adjudication outputs.

They should be used in the Master Manual for historical traceability, not as
unqualified current truth.
