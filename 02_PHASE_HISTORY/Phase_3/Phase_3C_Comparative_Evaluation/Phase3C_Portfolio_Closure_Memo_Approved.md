# Project mC — Phase 3C Retained-Portfolio Closure Memo

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 27 July 2026  
**Status:** Approved for project record

## Decision

Project mC will end Phase 3C architecture work with three retained concepts:

1. OpenBraille
2. VibeGuard
3. TrueMoist

TrustLatch and ColdTrace are deferred before completion of their concept-specific Phase 3C architecture work.

They are not classified as technically failed or permanently rejected.

## Why the portfolio is being narrowed

The three completed concepts already provide a sufficient decision set:

- OpenBraille: assistive hardware, social impact, and the highest long-term physical-invention ceiling.
- VibeGuard: strongest live embedded/DSP demonstration and strong team fit.
- TrueMoist: lowest overall engineering risk and a plausible compensation-method innovation path.

Continuing full Phase 3C for TrustLatch and ColdTrace would require additional research reports, synthesis, memories, PDR updates, source management and error auditing. The expected additional decision value is lower than the cognitive and schedule cost.

Explicit cybersecurity alignment is not a mandatory project-selection criterion. Patent-development potential is the higher strategic priority, subject to feasibility, budget, timeline, demonstrability and team capability.

## Status of deferred concepts

### TrustLatch

Deferred because its strongest value is cybersecurity and academic demonstration, while its patent landscape is comparatively crowded. Existing Phase 2 and Phase 3B findings remain preserved but dormant.

### ColdTrace

Deferred because it adds another sensing/model-validation workload, carries thermal-test and FTO complications, and does not currently provide a sufficient advantage over the retained three.

## Phase statement

Phase 3C is complete for the retained shortlist of OpenBraille, VibeGuard and TrueMoist.

It must not be described as completed for all five original concepts.

## Re-entry rule

TrustLatch or ColdTrace may be reactivated only when:

- all retained concepts fail a decisive feasibility or validation gate;
- stakeholder requirements materially change; or
- new evidence significantly improves their patent and implementation case.

## Immediate next action

Conduct one formal comparative down-selection among OpenBraille, VibeGuard and TrueMoist.

The comparison must prioritize:

1. credible patent-development potential;
2. feasibility within approximately two months;
3. budget compliance at ₹3,000 preferred and ₹5,000 maximum;
4. team capability;
5. strength and clarity of the final demonstration;
6. validation burden and failure risk;
7. potential to create a specific, defensible engineering contribution.

After selecting one concept, create:

- the chosen concept’s Engineering Reference Manual;
- the final implementation context pack;
- a cleaned NotebookLM active-source set;
- Phase 4 entry documents and validation gates.

## Source-control implication

TrustLatch and ColdTrace sources should remain archived and unchecked by default in NotebookLM. Their authoritative Phase 2/3B documents should not be deleted.
