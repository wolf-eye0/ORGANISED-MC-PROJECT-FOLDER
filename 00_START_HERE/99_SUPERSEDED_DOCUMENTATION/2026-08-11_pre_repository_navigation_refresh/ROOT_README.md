## What this repository is

Project mC is a patent-oriented embedded-systems research and development project. It records the progression from opportunity discovery and patent-landscape work through engineering feasibility, Phase 3C architecture definition, comparative decision support, teacher-facing preparation and controlled documentation.

The semester prototype is one milestone in a longer evidence-led process. A strong prototype, a useful publication result and a potentially patent-relevant later mechanism are different outcomes and must not be conflated.

## Current project status

- Phase 1A, Phase 1B, Phase 1B.5, Phase 1C, Phase 2, Phase 3A and Phase 3B are complete.
- Phase 3C architecture work is complete for the retained concepts: **OpenBraille, VibeGuard and TrueMoist**.
- **TrustLatch and ColdTrace are deferred**, not technically rejected; neither received a complete retained-concept Phase 3C architecture.
- **VibeGuard is the strongest/current internally recommended semester-build candidate.**

- PIRG, VPD-C7 and other future mechanisms remain hypothetical, confidential where appropriate, evidence-gated research proposals.

## Authority order

When records conflict, use the full authority map. The practical hierarchy is:

1. Engineering Design Review.
2. Latest accepted Project mC Decision Register—currently v1.2.
3. Approved Phase 3C Retained-Portfolio Closure Memo.
4. Final Phase 3C concept architecture reports.
5. Phase 3C memories.
6. Phase 3C SOPs.
7. Validated current down-selection evidence and test/uncertainty controls.
8. Earlier phase reports and memories in descending phase order.
9. Current patent-evidence syntheses and pre-build simulations as bounded supporting evidence.
10. Historical and raw independent research.

Authority is determined by content, control status and provenance—not filename recency, folder convenience or model agreement. Raw AI research is evidence, not authority. Final adjudications are comparative decision context, not votes. Upload copies and manifests do not gain technical authority by being newer.

## Concept status

### OpenBraille

Retained Phase 3C concept and the official zeroth-review project. Its frozen semester architecture is the macro-scale six-dot SG90/eccentric-cam/vertical-pin design. It remains valuable accessibility and mechatronics research. VPD-C7 is a separate hypothetical later research direction, not an implemented feature or proved invention.

### VibeGuard

Retained Phase 3C concept and the current internal semester recommendation. Its frozen semester MVP is the one-target-mounted-accelerometer, ESP32, local-processing Level-1 machine-vibration demonstration. PIRG and dense-machine attribution are separate future research and must not enter the semester scope silently.

### TrueMoist

Retained and fully evaluated Phase 3C concept with its validated 72-run architecture. It remains part of the comparative record, but it is not the present semester-build recommendation.

### TrustLatch

Deferred before a complete retained-concept Phase 3C architecture. It was not technically rejected and remains historical/deferred portfolio evidence.

### ColdTrace

Deferred before a complete retained-concept Phase 3C architecture. It was not technically rejected and remains historical/deferred portfolio evidence.

## Semester recommendation

VibeGuard is currently recommended because its frozen architecture offers the strongest combination of three-month feasibility, expected budget fit, team skill alignment, quantitative bench validation and clear live demonstration. This is an internal engineering recommendation—not teacher approval and not a patentability conclusion.

Until a real teacher decision is recorded, OpenBraille remains the formally presented zeroth-review concept and the requested change remains pending.

## Patent-development boundary

**Semester MVP ≠ established invention.**

- The frozen semester builds should be evaluated as engineering prototypes.
- PIRG, VPD-C7 and other post-semester mechanisms are hypothetical and unbuilt.
- Their continuation depends on physical experiments, kill criteria, refreshed primary-source searches, institutional IPR review and professional legal advice where appropriate.
- Prior syntheses, simulations, adjudications and model councils cannot substitute for claims, official legal status or experimental evidence.
