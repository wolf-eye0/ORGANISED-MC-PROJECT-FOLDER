# Project mC — Archive Index

## Superseded

Earlier architecture versions, decision-register variants and convenience-copy
folder contents are retained here for historical traceability.

## Exact duplicates

The organized workspace generally avoids storing exact duplicates. Duplicate
source paths are recorded in copy and coverage logs.

## Failed or partial research

Failed, fragmented, concept-swapped, partial and uncontrolled research variants
are isolated here so they cannot silently re-enter the valid evidence set.

## Legacy ZIPs

Original ZIP packages are preserved without being treated as canonical project
directories.

## Unrelated quarantine

Unrelated files are not copied. Their original paths and exclusion reasons are
recorded in `INTENTIONAL_EXCLUSIONS.md`.
