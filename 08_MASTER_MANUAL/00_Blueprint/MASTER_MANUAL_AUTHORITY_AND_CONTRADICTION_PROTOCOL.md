# Project mC — Authority and Contradiction Protocol

## Authority hierarchy

Use the following order whenever sources disagree:

1. Current teacher requirements and formally recorded approvals.
2. Project mC Decision Register v1.2.
3. Authoritative final Phase 3C architecture reports.
4. Final concept memories and SOPs.
5. Current comparative evidence, gates and uncertainty registers.
6. Valid patent-evidence syntheses and final adjudications.
7. Controlled independent research lanes.
8. Earlier phase reports and concept dossiers.
9. Launchers, manifests, transport bundles and archived material.

A lower source cannot silently override a higher source.

## Architecture identity lock

Each concept must retain its frozen identity:

- OpenBraille: six-servo macro-cell architecture.
- VibeGuard: ADXL345 plus ESP32 vibration-monitoring architecture.
- TrueMoist: validated local temperature and salinity-treatment compensation
  architecture.

Moving rollers, software-security substitutions, live-EC systems and other
concept swaps must not be reintroduced as though they were the frozen design.

## Model-output rule

Independent research lanes and syntheses are evidence sources, not votes.
Agreement among several models does not automatically establish truth.

## Missing-information rule

Do not reconstruct a missing conclusion from fragments. Mark it as unavailable
or partial.

## Patent-language rule

Use terms such as:

- patent hypothesis;
- potentially differentiating mechanism;
- candidate claim direction;
- requires prior-art verification;
- requires experimental evidence;
- requires institutional IPR review.

Do not promise patent grant, novelty, inventive step or freedom to operate.

## Current-build versus future-research rule

Every technical section must clearly separate:

1. frozen semester MVP;
2. allowed implementation refinements;
3. post-semester experimental research;
4. speculative patent-development path.

## Approval-state rule

A request, recommendation or adjudication does not prove teacher approval.
The manual must state the approval status only from an explicit controlling
record.

## Cost and specification rule

Historical prices must be dated. Final publication costing and hardware
specifications require a fresh market and manufacturer verification pass.

## Contradiction recording

Material contradictions must be recorded in a contradiction table containing:

- claim or design point;
- higher-authority source;
- conflicting source;
- resolution;
- residual uncertainty;
- required test or approval.
