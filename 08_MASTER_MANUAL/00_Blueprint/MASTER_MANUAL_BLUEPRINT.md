## Concept-status safeguards

- Folder placement does not itself finalize the semester concept.
- The manual must distinguish a proposed change from recorded teacher approval.
- OpenBraille, VibeGuard and TrueMoist architectures must never be combined into a hybrid design unless a later authorized decision explicitly creates one.
- Ordinary semester MVPs must not be described as patent-ready.
- D11, VPD-C7, PIRG and similar future paths remain hypotheses until supported by experiments and institutional IPR review.
- Pre-build simulations are conditional planning evidence, not proof that a later mechanism works.
