# Chapter 12 — Research Provenance and AI Workflow Control

## 12.1 Chapter purpose and evidence boundary

Chapter 12 should explain how Project mC used AI-assisted research, synthesis and adjudication while preserving source identity, concept fidelity, authority and human accountability. Its evidence boundary is procedural and evidentiary, not technical: it may describe how research lanes were commissioned, packaged, audited, combined and excluded, but it must not use manifests, launchers, repeated model outputs or workflow files to prove an architecture, performance result, approval, patent right or technical conclusion.

The repaired Chapter 12 packet contains seventeen representative source assignments:

- three representative input manifests;
- one representative launcher;
- three Tier 2 concept patent-evidence indices;
- one Tier 2 comparative authoritative-source manifest;
- one Stage 5 provenance audit; and

These records are sufficient to reconcile workflow roles, limitations, exclusions and disclosure requirements. They are not a substitute for the current architecture, decision or validation sources controlled in validated Parts I–V.

## 12.2 Project research and synthesis workflow

The controlled workflow can be represented as a sequence of evidence transformations:

1. **Project authority and frozen identity are established first.** The governing synthesis instructions require the relevant Engineering Design Review, Decision Register v1.2, approved portfolio closure, final architecture, final memory, final SOP and current evidence records to control concept identity. Independent research cannot redefine the concept.

2. **Independent research lanes are commissioned against a defined scope.** Concept-specific instructions define the evidence cut-off, required searches, concept-fidelity tripwires, source hierarchy, legal distinctions, expected output structure and prohibited claims.

3. **A physical package is assembled.** Manifests record exactly which governing, authority, historical and independent-research files were included. Reduced-file bundles may be created only to satisfy upload limits. The original separate sources remain authoritative.

4. **Independent synthesis configurations audit the lanes.** The OpenBraille and VibeGuard instructions expressly prohibit a synthesis from seeing, predicting or imitating another synthesis. The TrueMoist synthesis is likewise blind to prior-chat context, other syntheses and cross-concept ranking. The synthesis must audit concept fidelity, primary-source support, claim scope, status, legal distinctions, technical realism and completeness.

5. **Invalid sections or outputs are excluded rather than averaged.** Concept-swapped outputs, unsupported conclusions and incomplete fragments are downgraded or excluded. A strong minority source may control over several weak sources when its claim-level evidence is better.

6. **Controlled syntheses are selected.** Each retained concept has two valid syntheses. Separate failed or supplementary reports remain outside the controlled synthesis/adjudication set.

7. **Cross-concept adjudication uses the selected evidence.** The adjudication instructions require normalization of the frozen MVP and later research route, reliability auditing of each synthesis, separate analysis of semester buildability and post-MVP invention development, adversarial comparison, experiments and kill conditions. The adjudications are decision context, not votes and not approval records.

8. **Human-controlled architecture, testing and approval remain decisive.** AI-assisted outputs can identify leads, expose contradictions, suggest experiments and organize evidence. They cannot authorize a concept, validate physical performance, establish legal rights, assign inventorship or replace teacher/institutional decisions.

Project mC’s earlier PDR-041 language about preserving duplicate findings for confidence scoring must be read through the later and higher-control no-vote rule: recurrence is a search signal, not decision authority, and exact duplicates or transport variants have no additional evidentiary weight.

## 12.4 Model outputs as evidence, not votes

The controlling rule is:

> A model output is an auditable evidence source. Its value depends on concept fidelity, source traceability, primary support, claim-level reasoning, legal-status discipline, completeness and technical realism—not on the number of models that repeat it.

Accordingly:

- six model lanes do not create a six-member panel vote;
- two valid syntheses are not two votes;
- two final adjudications are not votes;
- a compiled follow-up from the same lane remains one lane;
- three fragments from one Mistral session remain one partial lane;
- a PDF and Markdown rendering of the same report do not create corroboration;
- a transport bundle containing copied source text is not new evidence;
- repeated unsupported patentability or FTO language does not become reliable through recurrence;
- a well-supported minority conclusion may override multiple weak conclusions;
- architecture facts are taken from the current authority set, not inferred from model consensus.

The concept indices apply this rule concretely. The failed OpenBraille Deep Research synthesis is “not valid evidence and is not a vote.” The failed VibeGuard High + Deep Research synthesis is not valid evidence. The supplementary TrueMoist Work Max audit is outside the controlled six-lane adjudication set.

## 12.5 Synthesis and adjudication roles

### Synthesis role

A concept synthesis is a controlled analytical transformation of project authority and independent research. It may:

- inventory the package;
- audit each lane’s fidelity and reliability;
- verify load-bearing patent and technical claims;
- distinguish facts, source assertions, inferences, recommendations and unresolved items;
- compare frozen features with prior art;
- separate novelty, inventive step, eligibility and FTO;
- propose experiments, baselines, ablations and kill conditions;
- state a conditional patent-development track.

It may not:

- override the frozen architecture;
- import another synthesis;
- infer missing conclusions;
- treat model agreement as truth;
- promise patentability, grant or FTO;
- substitute a different concept;
- establish physical performance that has not been tested.

### Adjudication role

The final cross-concept adjudication instructions define an independent review-board role. The board compares the selected, concept-faithful syntheses under the higher project authority and common evidence. It must audit the reliability of all six selected syntheses, normalize current MVP and future route, distinguish semester feasibility from post-MVP patent-development potential and identify the experiments and kill conditions that should govern later decisions.

The adjudications are controlled decision support. They can recommend VibeGuard for semester build and identify competing later research routes. They do not:

- prove teacher approval;
- replace Decision Register v1.2;
- amend a final architecture;
- authorize Phase 4, procurement or filing;
- establish that a proposed future mechanism works;
- create an inventorship determination.

## 12.6 Governing instructions

Governing instructions are workflow authority for the named task. They define what a model or reviewer was required to do, which files it was allowed to use, the source hierarchy, exclusions, concept-fidelity locks, evidence cut-off, required output and prohibited claims.

Representative controlled instructions establish the following:

- OpenBraille synthesis must preserve the final six-servo macro-cell architecture and treat Phase 2 only as historical search leads.
- VibeGuard synthesis must preserve passive single-node ADXL345/ESP32 vibration monitoring and exclude software-security, active cancellation, cloud predictive maintenance and other substitutions.
- TrueMoist synthesis must preserve the validated 72-run local compensation architecture and exclude Decision Register v1.1 and the later 135-run `TrueMoist/by claude/` files.
- Final adjudication must use Decision Register v1.2, the approved closure memo, common evidence and only the six selected syntheses; excluded concept swaps and the supplementary TrueMoist audit are outside the blind package.

An instruction proves the intended method and constraints. It does not prove that every downstream output complied, that the sources were factually correct, that searches were complete or that conclusions were validated. Compliance must be audited against the resulting report and authoritative project files.

## 12.10 Missing-conclusion and non-reconstruction rule

The non-reconstruction rule is mandatory:

- do not infer a final verdict from a partial report’s direction;
- do not combine unrelated fragments into a conclusion the source never gave;
- do not treat an appendix as a missing conclusion;
- do not use the majority view of other models to fill a missing section;
- do not assume a continuation existed because the report format suggests one;
- do not import a conclusion from prior-chat context or another synthesis;
- mark the item “partial,” “unavailable,” “unresolved” or “no final verdict.”

This rule protects both evidentiary integrity and authorship. Reconstructing a conclusion would create new model-authored content and falsely attribute it to the original lane.

### Concept-swap detection

Concept-fidelity tripwires require each report to restate the frozen concept and remain within its architecture boundary. A concept swap is not a minor error. It invalidates any inseparable conclusion derived from the substituted architecture.

- OpenBraille concept swap: moving-roller/encoder-wheel/RAMPS/Mega architecture substituted for the frozen six-servo macro cell.
- VibeGuard concept swap: artifact, configuration, secret and dependency scanners substituted for the frozen vibration-monitoring system.
- TrueMoist prohibited swaps include live-EC runtime sensing, universal/multi-soil systems, cloud or online adaptive learning and the superseded 135-run architecture where inconsistent with the current 72-run design.

Failed concept-swapped outputs remain archived as negative provenance and methodology lessons. They cannot be averaged, quoted as alternative evidence for the frozen concept or silently rehabilitated by extracting favorable passages.

## 12.12 Human verification and final accountability

Human control is required at every transition from information to decision:

- humans define the project requirements and approve governing instructions;
- humans freeze the architecture and record decisions;
- humans select and package the source set;
- humans audit concept fidelity and source quality;
- humans decide which outputs are valid, partial, failed or excluded;
- humans verify hardware, measurements, experiments and demonstrations;
- humans record teacher/institutional approval;
- humans determine whether institutional IPR or legal review should begin;
- human authors remain accountable for every final statement in the Master Manual.

The workflow expressly states that AI-generated suggestions are not inventorship. Workflow records do not establish who conceived a patent claim, who reduced it to practice, who authored a technical contribution or who should be named as an inventor. Contributor chronology, dated invention records, experiments and institutional review must be preserved separately. This reconciliation makes no inventorship or authorship determination.

## 12.13 AI-use disclosure and reproducibility requirements

A draft-safe AI-use disclosure for the final manual should state at least:

- AI systems were used for independent research, synthesis, adversarial audit, document organization and decision support.
- Named lanes and modes should be identified where controlled records exist.
- The governing instruction, evidence cut-off, source package and exclusions should be recorded.
- Model outputs were not treated as votes.
- Partial and failed outputs were retained with their status.
- Excluded outputs were not imported into controlled conclusions.
- Valid syntheses and adjudications remained subordinate to current project authority.
- Human authors reviewed and accepted responsibility for final claims.
- Technical performance and patent/legal conclusions require separate physical, official-source and institutional verification.

Minimum reproducibility package for an AI-assisted workflow:

1. governing instruction;
2. exact input manifest and source paths;
3. file hashes or immutable identifiers;
4. launcher or initiating prompt where relevant;
5. model/mode and date if recorded;
6. complete output without silent editing;
7. reliability and concept-fidelity assessment;
8. exclusion register;
9. selected synthesis/adjudication record;
10. contradiction and unresolved-item register;
11. human review record;
12. change log for later revisions.

Reproducibility means another reviewer can understand and inspect the process. It does not guarantee that the same model will produce identical prose or conclusions.

## 14.12 Authority, contradiction and approval boundaries

Source integrity and authority are different dimensions.

- A perfectly hashed obsolete file remains obsolete.
- A canonical file can contain unresolved or untested claims.
- A high-authority architecture can require physical validation.
- A teacher-facing presentation can be authentic without proving teacher approval.
- A change-request document can be authentic without proving acceptance.
- An adjudication can be authentic and complete without being a vote or final authority.
- A pre-build simulation can be hash-verified while remaining hypothetical.

The authority protocol must therefore be applied after identity is established. Contradictions must record the higher-authority source, conflicting source, resolution, residual uncertainty and required test/approval.
