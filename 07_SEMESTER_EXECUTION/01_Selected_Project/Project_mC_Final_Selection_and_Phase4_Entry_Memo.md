## 2. Purpose

This memo formally closes comparative semester-project selection and authorizes the transition into controlled VibeGuard semester implementation.

## 3. Decision history

OpenBraille was presented at the zeroth review. Project mC subsequently completed engineering, feasibility, architecture, comparative and patent-path work for the retained concepts. That work identified VibeGuard as the strongest semester-build recommendation. Earlier records correctly left the final concept decision pending because teacher authorization to make the change had not yet been established.

The project leader now reports that the teacher verbally delegated the final concept choice to the team and stated that the team may choose whichever project it prefers. No written teacher approval, signature, email, institutional memo or quotation is claimed. The teacher did not personally select VibeGuard. After receiving that delegation, the team accepted the Project mC evaluation and selected VibeGuard.

## 4. Final semester selection

**VIBEGUARD IS THE FINAL PROJECT mC SEMESTER IMPLEMENTATION.**

This is a final team selection for current execution, not merely a recommendation.

## 5. Basis for selection

The controlled selection basis is:

- lower coupled implementation risk;
- stronger fit for the technical team;
- lower expected cost and more budget headroom;
- easier quantitative validation;
- stronger schedule recovery;
- a clear live demonstration; and
- strong continuation into post-semester experimental research.

VibeGuard was not selected because a patent is guaranteed. Selection makes no novelty, patentability, freedom-to-operate, filing or grant conclusion.

## 6. Frozen semester architecture

The Phase 4 Level-1 architecture is frozen as:

- one rigidly mounted ADXL345-class tri-axis accelerometer;
- ESP32-WROOM-32 or authorized ESP32-S3;
- SPI;
- local preprocessing;
- DC removal;
- RMS plus persistence as the mandatory baseline;
- triggered FFT and selected band features;
- ordinary Mahalanobis as an optional comparator only;
- RGB local state;
- USB logging;
- one guarded low-voltage motor/fan test rig; and
- controlled normal versus induced abnormal vibration.

Final selection does not authorize architecture drift.

## 7. Explicit Phase 4 exclusions

The semester MVP excludes:

- PIRG;
- a permanent second accelerometer;
- dense-machine source attribution;
- active cancellation;
- cloud dependency;
- industrial remaining-useful-life or failure-prediction claims; and
- patent-performance claims.

## 8. Status of other concepts

**OpenBraille:** Retained as a controlled accessibility/mechatronics and future-research record. It is not rejected as invalid and is not selected for semester implementation.

**TrueMoist:** Retained as a controlled experimental/research record. It is not selected for semester implementation.

**TrustLatch and ColdTrace:** Remain deferred under existing controls and are not technically rejected.

## 9. Phase closure

**PHASE 3C — CLOSED.**

Architecture work for the retained shortlist and final comparative selection have served their purpose. Phase 3C must not be reopened unless a later controlling decision explicitly requires reselection because of catastrophic feasibility failure or materially changed requirements.

**PHASE 4 — OPEN / AUTHORIZED FOR IMPLEMENTATION PREPARATION AND EXECUTION.**

- **Phase 4A —** Team onboarding, execution planning, evidence-system setup and procurement.
- **Phase 4B —** Component acceptance and bench bring-up.

- **Phase 4D —** DSP, data collection and controlled validation.

## 11. Phase 4 entry conditions

Before physical purchasing or building proceeds, the team must:

- verify the current bill of materials and prices;
- choose the exact approved ESP32 development board;
- accept a specific ADXL345 breakout only after inspection and testing;
- confirm motor and guard safety;
- establish engineering logs;
- freeze team roles; and
- preserve configuration and raw-data history.

These conditions control execution quality. They do not reopen concept selection. Phase 4 entry authorizes procurement preparation; it does not record procurement as completed.

## 12. Evidence and engineering-log requirement

Every material item must be recorded traceably, including each:

- hardware change;
- firmware change;
- sensor mount change;
- experiment;
- failure;
- successful milestone;
- engineering decision;
- repair;
- dataset; and
- final validation result.

Negative results must not be deleted. Raw experimental evidence must not be overwritten.

## 13. Future patent/research boundary

- VibeGuard Level 1 is the semester baseline, not a patent-ready invention.
- PIRG is only the first currently identified post-MVP invention-development candidate.
- PIRG failure does not automatically end VibeGuard research.
- Real failures or limitations discovered during Phase 4 or later work may motivate other future VibeGuard mechanisms.
- Any future filing requires fresh prior-art research, experiments, inventorship and disclosure records, and institutional and professional IPR review.

No patent grant, novelty, freedom-to-operate or filing outcome is claimed.
