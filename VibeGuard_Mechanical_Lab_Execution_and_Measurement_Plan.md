## 3. What to Request from the Mechanical Lab

When entering the workshop, request the following specific equipment:

| Item | Technical Specification | Why It Is Crucial |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Digital / Vernier Caliper** | 0–150 mm range, $0.01\text{ mm}$ resolution | Essential for measuring N20 D-shaft flat, ADXL hole pitch, and screw shanks. |
| **2. Pillar / Bench Drill Press** | Fixed vertical quill drill press | **Never use a handheld drill** on acrylic. Handheld drills wobble, drill crooked holes, and crack the brittle plastic. |
| **3. High-Speed Steel (HSS) Bits** | **2.5 mm, 3.0 mm, and 3.2 mm** | • 2.5 mm: Pilot hole.<br>• 3.2 mm: Standard through-hole clearance for M3 screws. |
| **4. Clamping & Backing Block** | Machine vise + scrap wood/MDF block | Place scrap wood *directly under* the acrylic when drilling. It supports the plastic so the bit breaks through without blowing out the back. |
| **5. Surface Marking Supplies** | Paper masking tape + sharp scriber / punch | Stick masking tape over the acrylic before marking. It prevents the drill bit from skating/slipping across the smooth plastic. |
| **6. Deburring / Countersink Tool** | Hand deburring blade or 90° bit | Gently chamfers the hole rims to relieve mechanical stress and prevent spiderweb micro-cracks. |

## 5. Option 3: Window Cutout Mechanical Blueprint

### Why Option 3 is Selected
The 8-pin male header is soldered with the pins pointing out the **back** of the ADXL345 PCB, and the DuPont female ribbon cable plugs into the rear. Placing this board against a flat solid plastic wall would cause the DuPont connector to bump against the wall.

### The Engineering Solution (Window Cutout)
Instead of adding loose standoffs (which reduce vibration rigidity) or flipping the board around (which hides component markings), **Option 3 incorporates a rectangular clearance window** into the 3D-printed bracket (`FAB-02`).

```text
               FAB-02 UPRIGHT WALL WITH WINDOW CUTOUT (FRONT VIEW)
               
                    X = -12 mm                           X = +12 mm
                    ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐  Z = 22 mm
                    │                                     │
                    │   ┌─────────────────────────────┐   │
                    │   │                             │   │
                    │   │    WINDOW CLEARANCE CUTOUT  │   │  Z = 13 mm to 21 mm
                    │   │   (DuPont Cable Passes      │   │  (Width: 18.0 mm)
                    │   │    Through into Rear)       │   │
                    │   │                             │   │
                    │   └─────────────────────────────┘   │
                    │                                     │
                    │       (O)                 (O)       │  Z = 7.0 mm (M3 Holes)
                    │   X = -7.5 mm         X = +7.5 mm   │  (15.0 mm Pitch)
                    └───────┬─────────────────────┬───────┘  Z = 0 mm
                            │                     │
                    ════════╧═════════════════════╧════════  Base Foot (to Acrylic)
```

### Physical Mounting Sequence:
1. The lower half of the ADXL345 PCB clamps directly and rigidly to the lower wall using two M3 $\times$ 10 mm screws through the $15.0\text{ mm}$ pitch holes.
2. The upper half of the PCB overlaps the **Clearance Window**.
3. The 8-pin header and black DuPont connector pass straight through the window into open space behind the bracket.
4. **Vibration Transmission**: Mechanical vibration transmits through the solid bolted lower half directly from the baseplate into the sensor silicon without flex, wobble, or resonance damping.
